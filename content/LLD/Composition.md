mental models to remember:

Dependency
"I USE you."

Association
"I KNOW you."

Aggregation
"I HAVE you, but you are independent."

Composition
"I OWN you."

Inheritance
"I AM a type of you."

Realization
"I IMPLEMENT your contract."
